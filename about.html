The company behind the platform.

EMC General is developed and operated by Lightning Shop LLC, a Florida limited liability company established on April 8, 2022. EMC General is the company’s commerce and services platform in development.

Our mission.

Finding the right product and finding someone to install it often happen in separate places. We want to bring those steps together so customers can move from a project idea to suitable supplies and relevant professional help.

Led by Eliad Camacho.

Eliad Camacho leads the development of EMC General. Our focus is on a practical marketplace experience that connects customer needs with products and local expertise.

What we’re building.

Our product roadmap combines product discovery, local professional connections, and a planned AI project assistant. The assistant is intended to respond to questions, clarify project needs, and help customers explore suitable products and service providers.

EMC General is currently pre-launch. The public website is a marketplace preview; purchasing, professional booking, and AI assistance are not yet available.
